# IMMORTAL — Socioeconomic Experimental Premise

**Status:** Public explanatory premise / non-normative  
**Date:** September 2026

## The question

> **What happens when economic coordination is designed from explicit shared principles rather than from a single proprietary objective?**

IMMORTAL is an experiment built around that question. The philosophical premise is deliberately left open for further development; the socioeconomic experiment is already more concrete: the project defines observable rules, incentives, participation mechanisms and economic constraints that can be tested in an operating system.

This question is not presented as a theorem, a prediction, or a claim that one economic order is inherently superior to another. It is the starting hypothesis for a system designed from explicit principles and open to observation, criticism and revision.

## IMMORTAL is not born from blockchain

IMMORTAL is a universal, chain-agnostic economic protocol.

It is not designed specifically for blockchain, and blockchain is not a prerequisite for its economic semantics. The protocol defines economic meaning, constraints, obligations, admissible transitions and safety principles independently of the execution environment.

**IMMORTAL is blockchain-agnostic because it was not born from blockchain.**

A blockchain is therefore one possible realization environment: a new frontier from which different economic possibilities can be explored through concrete systems.

## Not capitalism on a blockchain

IMMORTAL is not an attempt to move an existing capitalist economic model onto a blockchain.

Its starting point is instead a principles-first architecture:

- open-source development;
- no privileged economic authority outside the protocol's principles;
- no central owner whose role is to extract value from the protocol itself;
- explicit and inspectable economic rules;
- shared participation in the evolution of the system;
- separation between universal principles, execution infrastructure and application policy.

These principles do not guarantee a particular social outcome. They define the experimental conditions.

The hypothesis is that, when economic coordination is constrained by shared principles rather than by the interests of a proprietary economic authority, different incentive structures may emerge.

Those structures could favor forms of cooperation, shared development and collective long-term interest alongside, or instead of, purely competitive forms of coordination.

Whether that actually happens is an empirical question.

## Many environments, one economic constitution

IMMORTAL can be realized through multiple adapters:

```text
                    IMMORTAL
          universal economic principles
                         |
        +----------------+----------------+
        |                |                |
        v                v                v
   Cardano Adapter   Bitcoin Adapter   Other Adapter
        |                |                |
        v                v                v
     Cardano          Bitcoin         Environment X
        |                |                |
        v                v                v
     PRE-RICH         DApp Y            DApp Z
```

The important condition is not technical compatibility alone.

An adapter is acceptable only when the concrete environment can realize the applicable IMMORTAL semantics **without altering its principles, introducing a new economic authority, or silently weakening the properties that IMMORTAL requires**.

The general rule is:

> **Many environments, one economic constitution.**

An Adapter is therefore a translator between IMMORTAL and a particular execution world.

It translates abstract, economically admissible requirements into concrete operations that the environment can realize, and translates observations and evidence from that environment back into forms that IMMORTAL can evaluate.

The Adapter does not become a second economic engine.

## PRE-RICH as the first experiment

PRE-RICH is a Cardano-native DApp and the first concrete application of IMMORTAL.

It belongs to the execution environment in which it is built while adopting IMMORTAL as its economic/protocol model.

PRE-RICH can express application intent and strategy. IMMORTAL determines the economically admissible space. The Cardano Adapter translates that admissible space into realizations that Cardano can execute and reports the resulting observations and evidence back to the protocol.

The first application is therefore not the definition of the protocol.

It is one of the first places in which the protocol's hypothesis can be observed in an operating system.

## What this experiment is asking

The experiment is not:

> How do we put the existing economy onto a blockchain?

It is:

> **What becomes possible when we start from different principles?**

IMMORTAL does not assume the answer.

It creates a framework in which the question can be explored.
