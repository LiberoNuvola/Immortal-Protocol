Economic Algorithm

Truth → safe action → state.

The canonical sequence turns economic reasoning into an explicit transition boundary. Each stage exists to prevent an implicit assumption from becoming hidden authority.

01 · TRUTH

Authoritative inputs are identified.

02 · CANONICAL STATE

The state used for the decision is fixed.

03 · OBLIGATIONS

Existing and declared future commitments are protected.

04 · EXECUTABLE LIQUIDITY

Only actually usable resources enter the decision.

05 · Ω

Relevant uncertainty and environmental shocks are represented.

06 · VIABILITY

The resulting state must remain inside the declared viable region.

07 · SAFE ACTIONS

Only actions satisfying the gate remain admissible.

08 · POLICY

Governance and profile rules constrain the allowed transition.

09 · ATOMIC TRANSITION

The action and its state change are committed together.

10 · STATE HISTORY

The resulting state becomes auditable history.

Core distinction

Safety does not imply liveness.

The current verification corpus explicitly separates safety, viability and liveness. A safe stall can still occur when required external inputs are unavailable; liveness claims require environmental and actor assumptions and are not silently inferred from the economic gate.
