What is IMMORTAL?

Define. Execute. Verify.

IMMORTAL is a universal protocol model for economic systems whose rules can be described through explicit state, obligations, safety conditions, admissible transitions and verifiable evidence.

01 · DEFINE

Make the rules explicit.

Economic state, obligations, safety conditions and admissible actions become explicit objects.

02 · EXECUTE

Turn rules into transitions.

Adapters translate the universal model into concrete execution environments without adding economic authority.

03 · VERIFY

Check what actually happened.

Implementation and deployment evidence are evaluated against the applicable specifications.

Protocol Pulse

Five dimensions. Different evidence states.

IMMORTAL does not compress research, implementation and deployment into one score. Each dimension carries its own evidence boundary.

ECONOMIC SAFETYMODEL
Defined

Universal economic semantics, protected obligations and admissibility are specified at the protocol layer.

CANONICAL STATEIMPLEMENTATION
Bounded

Canonical V3 state and conformance surfaces exist; correspondence evidence remains an explicit workstream.

EXECUTIONCARDANO
Active

Cardano is the current execution environment, with native-ledger and Preprod evidence still being closed.

EXTERNAL CANONICITYB3
Open evidence

Materios finality, authority provenance and authenticated state remain deployment-specific proof boundaries.

LIVENESSENVIRONMENT
Not claimed

Safety does not imply progress. Actor, substrate and input-availability assumptions require separate evidence.
