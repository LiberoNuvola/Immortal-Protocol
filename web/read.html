01 · WHY

Make economic rules explicit.

State, obligations, protection, admissibility and transitions are explicit objects rather than hidden assumptions.

02 · HOW

Separate authority from execution.

IMMORTAL defines economic semantics. An adapter realizes them on a concrete chain. An application supplies its own profile policy.

03 · PROOF

Evidence is part of the claim.

Specification, implementation, conformance and live execution are different evidence classes.
