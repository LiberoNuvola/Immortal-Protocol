IMMORTAL is the universal economic layer. Independent applications consume the protocol semantics and interfaces relevant to their own domain. PRE-RICH is the first application; Arb Agent is a separate experimental wallet companion. Neither application defines or controls the other. Boundary rule

Components can inform a transition without becoming its authority.

A Treasury observation does not redefine Genesis semantics. An Oracle does not become economic truth merely by publishing a price. A Materios RPC does not become canonicality merely by answering. A Beacon publisher does not become publisher-independent B3. The evidence layer records what was demonstrated; it does not promote it.
