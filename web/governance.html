Governance · authority boundary

Governance can evolve rules. It cannot bypass them.

IMMORTAL treats governance as an explicit protocol layer. Authorization, execution, observation and replay are kept distinct so a governance event can be reconstructed rather than trusted merely because an operator says it happened.

01

Authorization

Who or what is permitted to propose or authorize a decision?

02

Execution

What concrete transition was actually performed?

03

Observation

What evidence was observed from the execution environment?

04

Replay

Can an independent verifier reconstruct the same canonical decision?

Economic boundary

Governance is not an economic side door.

Governance cannot rewrite crystallized payouts, bypass the Economic Gate, create privileged Treasury entitlement, or silently alter canonical randomness. Any concrete governance act still requires the applicable execution and evidence package.
