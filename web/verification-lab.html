METHOD

Every claim needs a traceable boundary.

Follow the chain from source to implementation to execution. If a link is missing, the claim remains at the strongest layer actually supported.

Evidence ladder

Do not collapse the layers.

Specification→Formal result→Implementation→Conformance→Preprod→External witness

Each step answers a different question. Passing an earlier step does not establish the later one.
