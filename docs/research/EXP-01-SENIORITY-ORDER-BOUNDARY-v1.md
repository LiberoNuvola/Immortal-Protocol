# IMMORTAL — EXP-01 Seniority Order Boundary v1

**Classification:** research / conformance boundary, non-normative  
**Branch:** `work/b3-materios-provenance-evidence`  
**Date:** 2026-10-11

## Purpose

Extend EXP-01 from named ProtectedCapital buckets toward an explicit ordering boundary for typed obligations, without choosing a universal seniority taxonomy or payment-priority rule.

## Candidate boundary

A typed obligation ordering must carry an explicit ordering declaration.

The boundary requires:

1. obligation identity is explicit;
2. obligation type/bucket is explicit;
3. seniority rank is explicit when ordering is required;
4. equal ranks do not acquire an unstated tie-break;
5. missing ordering information is not converted into an arbitrary priority;
6. the ordering remains distinct from the amount/protection calculation.

No universal rank numbers or priority classes are defined.

## Open questions

- which obligation types are universally meaningful;
- whether seniority is total or partial ordering;
- how equal-priority obligations share settlement;
- interaction with term horizons and netting;
- whether a profile may declare additional protected classes.
