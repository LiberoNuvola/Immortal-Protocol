# IMMORTAL — Contributor Credential Transferability Independence Boundary v1

**Classification:** research / non-normative
**Branch:** `work/b3-materios-provenance-evidence`

## Purpose

Isolate credential ownership/transfer semantics from the economic and governance meaning of the underlying contribution.

Transferability remains **OPEN**. This boundary therefore does not decide whether a future credential is transferable or non-transferable.

## Boundary

A credential's holder identity and its contribution identity are distinct coordinates.

A transfer, if an authorized future rule permits one, must not by itself:

- change the canonical ContributionRecord;
- manufacture reward eligibility;
- manufacture a reward budget;
- create governance authority;
- create Treasury authority;
- change the evidence or rule version.

Conversely, a future non-transferability rule may reject the transfer without changing the underlying contribution record.

## Promotion

Passing this boundary proves only independence between ownership/transfer state and the semantic authority of the contribution record.

Canonical transferability, custody, revocation, supersession and any utility attached to ownership remain open.

**Status: RESEARCH / BOUNDARY ONLY.**
