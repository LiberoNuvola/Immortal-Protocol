**Classification:** research / conformance boundary, non-normative

**Date:** 2026-10-11

## Purpose

Investigate transfer of an already established obligation from one responsible party/context to another without accidentally creating or deleting economic obligation.

## Candidate boundary

A transfer candidate should preserve:

- explicit obligation identity;
- source and destination identity;
- the obligation's established amount/terms;
- provenance and history of the transfer;
- total economic conservation across the transfer.

A transfer is not a new claim, payout, or source of surplus merely because ownership/responsibility changes.

If the transfer authorization or identity relation is missing, the boundary remains **UNVERIFIABLE** rather than inventing a counterparty or completing the transfer implicitly.

## Open questions

- What constitutes transferable obligation identity?
- Who authorizes novation?
- Must source and destination both attest?
- Which terms may remain immutable during transfer?
- How are ProtectedCapital assignments moved?
- Can partial transfers exist, and under what independently governed rule?

No production novation mechanism is introduced.
