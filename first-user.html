Important distinction

“It worked” is not the same as “it is proven.”

A wallet can sign a transaction. A transaction can reach a ledger. A test can pass. None of these facts alone certifies the complete IMMORTAL economic or external-canonicality claim. The journey therefore ends in evidence, not in a green button.
