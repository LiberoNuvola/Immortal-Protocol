Understand · project evolution

A protocol is also
a sequence of evidence.

This timeline follows the project's architectural progression without treating historical implementation as permanent authority. Current status remains defined by the repository's present source hierarchy and evidence.

01
FOUNDATION

Constitution & economic model

Universal protocol semantics, economic safety boundaries and the source-of-truth hierarchy establish the normative layer.

02
V3

Canonical state & transitions

The protocol model evolves around explicit state, obligations, viability, safe action and atomic transition semantics.

03
REFERENCE

Conformance boundary

The Reference Adapter provides a deterministic executable comparison surface rather than redefining the economic kernel.

04
CARDANO

Concrete execution

The Cardano Adapter materializes the chain-specific boundary: UTxOs, Plutus, transaction construction, signing and observation.

05
PRE-RICH

First application

PRE-RICH instantiates the universal protocol with application-specific Scratch & Win policy and lifecycle.

06
PREPROD

Execution & evidence

Real Preprod execution becomes the next proof boundary: observed transactions, state and exact execution context.

07OPEN FRONT
EXTERNAL CANONICITY

B3 / independent witness

Publisher-independent Beacon canonicality and Materios finality/state-authentication evidence remain explicit future gates.

Read the timeline correctly

History explains the path. It does not certify the present.

A commit, prototype or previous deployment can explain how the system evolved. Current certification still depends on the current source, exact implementation and applicable evidence.
